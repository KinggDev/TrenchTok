What does the coin pay for?

Eight things. Every spend is posted.

Edits.

Hours in the edit room. Cuts, captions, transitions and the final render.

Sound.

Licensed tracks and sound design, so edits stay up and do not get muted.

Footage.

Charts, clips and screen recordings bought or made for a specific edit.

Boosts.

Paid reach on edits that already work. Nothing gets boosted before it earns views.

Burns.

Coins bought back and destroyed when a milestone hits. They leave the supply for good.

Collabs.

Paying other creators to make edits with the page. Every deal is listed by name.

Picks.

Holders vote on the next coin the page covers. The winner gets the next edit.

Reserve.

Whatever is not spent stays in the reserve and carries over to the next week.

Every edit is on the record.

The questions we can't skip.

Is this financial advice?

No. The page makes edits about memecoins, and memecoins can go to zero. Buy only what you are fine losing, and never because an edit told you to.

Does the creator hold coins?

The creator wallet is listed on the record page with its full balance. Any sale from it is posted before it happens, not after.

Who decides what gets spent?

The page decides spending on edits. Holders vote on which coin gets covered next. Both show up on the record with the amount and the date.

What happens if the page stops posting?

The reserve stays where it is, and the record shows that too. A page that stops posting has nothing to spend on.

That is the whole of it: a page that makes edits about coins, paid for by its own coin, with every spend out in the open.
